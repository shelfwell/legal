Shelfwell Support

Need help with Shelfwell or have a question about the app? Reach out using the contact below and you'll hear back from us.

Contact

Email: shelfwell@gmail.com

Response time is within a few days. When reporting a bug, please include your phone model, iOS or Android version, and a description of what happened. The quickest way is Settings > Contact the founder in the app, which fills in those details for you.

Common Questions

Do I need an account?
Yes, it takes a few seconds: continue with Apple, or with a code sent to your email. It keeps your items safe if you change phone, and it's how your team works together. Sign in on another phone with the same Apple ID or email to see the same items.

How do I add my team?
Open Settings > Team > Invite. Send the link, or read out the 6-character code. Codes work for 7 days. Your teammates can use iPhone or Android.

How do labels and scanning work?
Every item and folder has a QR label you can print on sticker sheets or a label printer. Scan a label in the app's Scan tab to open the item, add or remove stock, or check it out. You can also link existing barcodes to items.

Does it work without internet?
Yes. Everything works offline, including scanning. Changes sync automatically when you're back online.

I'm moving from a spreadsheet or another app. Can I import?
Yes. Export a CSV from your spreadsheet or current app, then use Settings > Import. The app maps the columns for you and shows a preview before anything is added. A Sortly export imports directly, with its folders, tags, notes, minimum levels, prices and photos.

What's free?
Everything, for up to 200 items and one person. Pro removes the item limit; Team also covers up to 5 people for one price. Export is always free.

Can I get my data out?
Yes, any time and on every plan: Settings > Export CSV gives you a file of all your items. We recommend exporting regularly and keeping the file somewhere safe, as your own backup.

How do subscriptions and refunds work?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID or Google account. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions. Refund requests go to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com or to Google Play.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Settings > Account > Delete account. If you own a team that still has members, the team and its items are passed to another member instead of being deleted.
