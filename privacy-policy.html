Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Controller / Developer: Federico Balzi, an individual sole developer domiciled in Italy (the "Developer", "we", "us", or "our"). Contact: shelfwell@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, used, and disclosed in connection with the mobile application currently distributed under the name "Shelfwell" (the "App"), a stock and equipment tracker for individuals and small teams. By downloading, installing, or using the App you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Guiding Principle - Only What the App Needs

We collect only the data needed to run the App: to store your items, keep them in sync across your devices and your team, send the alerts you turn on, and manage your subscription. We do not sell your personal data. We do not use advertising, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics.

The App works on your device first. Your data is stored locally and, so that it can be backed up, restored, and shared with your team, it is synchronised with our cloud backend, which is operated for us by the service providers listed in section 4.

2. Information We Process

(a) Account. When you first open the App, it creates an anonymous account identified only by a random user ID, so your data can be synced without asking you to sign in. If you later choose to sign in (for example to invite teammates, join a team, buy a subscription, or use the App on another device), we receive the details of the method you choose: with Sign in with Apple, a private user identifier and, only if you choose to share them, your name and an email address (which may be a private relay address); with Google, your name, email address, and profile picture link as provided by Google; with email sign-in, your email address. We never receive your Apple or Google password.

(b) Your workspace content. The items, folders, quantities, units, minimum levels, prices, notes, tags, custom fields, barcodes, and short label codes you create, and the photos you add, are stored on your device and in our cloud backend. Photos are compressed on your device before upload and kept in private storage that only members of your workspace can access.

(c) Team and activity information. If you share a workspace, its members can see its content, each member's display name and role, who currently has an item checked out (a member, or a name typed in as free text), and a history of changes showing who changed what and when. Please only enter other people's names where needed to track who has an item.

(d) Camera and photo library. The App uses the camera to scan QR codes and barcodes and to take photos of items, and the photo library to let you pick existing photos. Scanning happens on your device; camera frames are not recorded or uploaded. Only photos you choose to save to an item are stored and synced.

(e) Notifications. If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device so we can send low-stock alerts to the members of your workspace who have alerts turned on. An alert includes the item's name and quantity.

(f) Purchases and subscriptions. Subscriptions are sold through Apple's App Store or Google Play under your Apple ID or Google account. We do not receive or store your payment card details or billing address. Our subscription provider receives your App user ID, the ID of the workspace the subscription is for, and the purchase and renewal records needed to confirm whether your subscription is active.

(g) Diagnostics. If the App crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report (device model, operating system and App version, and technical details of the error) so we can fix bugs. We configure crash reporting not to send personal identifiers. Separately, if you have opted in via your device settings, Apple or Google may share aggregated crash and performance data with us.

(h) Imports, exports, and sharing. When you import a file, it is read on your device; if it contains photo links, the App downloads those photos from the addresses in the file. Exports (CSV files, PDF registers, labels, reorder lists) are created on your device and go only where you choose to send them through your device's share sheet.

3. What We Do Not Do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not use trackers, cookies, third-party SDKs for analytics or advertising, or the Advertising Identifier (IDFA). We do not build advertising profiles about you. We do not use your data or photos to train machine-learning models. We do not look at your workspace content except where needed to provide support you ask for, to fix a technical problem, or where required by law.

4. Service Providers

We use the following providers to run the App. They process data only on our instructions and to provide their service:

(i) Supabase, Inc.: database, sign-in, file storage, and server functions (our cloud backend), hosted in the European Union; (ii) Apple Inc. and Google LLC: sign-in, in-app purchases, and delivery of push notifications; (iii) RevenueCat, Inc.: subscription management; (iv) Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry): crash reporting; (v) 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo): relaying push notifications to Apple and Google.

Apple's privacy policy is available at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ and Google's at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Legal Bases (EEA / UK Users)

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) or the UK GDPR applies to you, we rely on the following legal bases: (i) performance of a contract with you (providing the App, syncing your data, team features, and subscriptions); (ii) our legitimate interests in keeping the App secure and reliable, fixing bugs, and preventing fraud, provided your interests and fundamental rights do not override these; and (iii) your consent, where we ask for it (for example, when your device asks permission to use the camera, photos, or notifications). You may withdraw consent at any time in your device settings.

If you use the App on behalf of a business or organisation, that organisation decides what is stored in its workspace and who can see it.

6. Retention and Deletion

We keep your account and workspace data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the App (Settings > Account > Delete account). When you do: workspaces where you are the only member are deleted together with their items and photos; workspaces you own that have other members are passed to another member so the team keeps its data; and you are removed from any other workspaces. Content you added to a shared workspace stays with that team. Signing out removes the App's data from that device. Data may remain in encrypted backups for a limited period before it is overwritten. Crash reports are deleted automatically after a limited period.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal data about you, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You can export your items at any time from the App (CSV export is always free) and delete your account from the App. For anything else, contact us at shelfwell@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time and in any event within the timeframes required by applicable law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. International Transfers

The Developer is located in Italy, and our main backend is hosted in the European Union. Some providers listed in section 4 are based in the United States and may process data there. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Children

The App is not intended for and must not be used by anyone under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect any information from persons under 16. If you believe a person under 16 has used the App, please contact us and we will take steps to delete any information we hold.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit between the App and our backend. Access to each workspace is restricted on the server to its members, and photos are kept in private storage. No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we will update the "Last updated" date and, where reasonably possible, notify you in-app. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
